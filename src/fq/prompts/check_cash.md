4. **Net cash.** This company holds net cash (cash and short-term investments less all debt, on the shareholders' share) of at least {{CASH_MIN}}% of its market value: the one figure over a price you are given, because so much idle cash is itself a question. Why is it not used? A company that can reinvest at a high return on capital should; one that cannot should pay it out; one whose shares trade below what they are worth should buy them back. Look for the catch: cash trapped abroad or blocked by capital controls; regulatory, customer or deposit money that is not really the owners'; a controlling shareholder or parent that keeps it or takes it out on its own terms; pending liabilities, litigation, guarantees or a large planned outlay; a record of spending cash badly. Say whether the cash is really available to minority shareholders and whether it is likely to be used well. Cash held for no stated reason, by owners who do not need minority shareholders, counts against the company.
